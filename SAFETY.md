class HAKIM safety policy

## Scope
- Educational and professional research support only
- No autonomous diagnosis or prescription
- No emergency triage decisions
- Patient-identifying information must not be submitted
- Every clinical claim must include a source and evidence limitation

## Uploaded data
- Do not process images or documents until consent, secure upload, retention, and deletion controls are implemented
- Upload review logs must not contain patient identifiers
- AI-generated imaging observations require clinician verification
